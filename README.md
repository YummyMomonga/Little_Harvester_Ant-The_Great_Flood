# Little_Harvester_Ant-The_Great_Flood
A ant game where you collect materials to build your home
## About
Little Harvester Ant: The Great Flood is a timed-collect-a-thon game where you follow in the shoes of a harvester ant facing their Rainstorm. You have to help your colony survive by foraging resources before the great floods happens. Goal: Survive the rainstorm. Objective: Collect materials and resources before time runs out... And don’t die. You play by using WASD or arrow keys to move your ant and collect a variety of items. However, a little guy like you is highly susceptible to environmental dangers. So watch out! Find out whenever you’d survive as a smol ant.
## Features
- Coolest 2D pixel graphics
- Very intense music (diegetic + nondiegetic) which follows the game progression
- Diverse range of items to collect
- Three levels with strict time limit, varying from easy, medium and hard.
- Immersive storyline from the Harvester’s ant perspective (win and lose endings for each levels)
